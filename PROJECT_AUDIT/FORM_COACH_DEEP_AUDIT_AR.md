# Form Coach — Deep Technical + Functional Audit

## Scope and final decision

This audit reviewed the current camera → frame pacing → on-device pose inference → smoothing → readiness → metrics → rep state → rules → feedback path. No new feature or exercise was added.

The conservative result is intentional: **no current exercise emits a form-warning rule after this audit**. The existing rules used fixed angles/baselines without real-user calibration or a view-specific biomechanical validation set. They were disabled rather than defended.

## Pipeline audit

- **Camera:** medium resolution, audio disabled, one stream owned by the Form Coach route.
- **Backpressure:** one frame/inference in flight; incoming frames are dropped while busy or before the adaptive interval.
- **Pacing:** target 15 FPS, minimum usable 7 FPS, slow inference increases the interval up to 200 ms.
- **Pose:** ML Kit stream mode, on-device only. The model output is converted to normalized landmark points; image bytes are not retained.
- **Smoothing:** One Euro filters for point coordinates plus visibility smoothing.
- **Readiness:** required landmarks, frame bounds, torso-scale distance proxy, camera view proxy, visibility, brightness, subject stability, and FPS. A failing quality issue now blocks live judgement too, not only initial setup.
- **Occlusion:** missing/low-confidence driver cannot advance the rep; any gap during an active cycle poisons that cycle so returning to the top cannot create a false rep.
- **Rep state:** top → excursion → bottom → return-to-top. A full rep now requires the configured bottom zone, minimum amplitude, minimum duration, and a continuous usable path. A half rep cannot become full only from amplitude.
- **Rules:** the nine previous rules were removed from active profiles: biceps (2), squat (3), and lateral raise (4). Their fixed thresholds were not validated enough to justify warnings.
- **Audio:** FormCuePlayer remains available for approved cues such as set completion. No active form-warning cue is emitted after the audit.
- **Lifecycle:** background/interruption pauses the stream and returns the engine to setup; route disposal stops the stream, disposes the camera/detector, closes the frame stream, stops TTS, and releases the optional recorder.
- **Persistence/network:** no API, database, or workout-engine call occurs during live analysis. Recording is disabled. Only in-memory pose points and snapshots exist during the screen session.

## What the current system can detect

- Whether enough required landmarks are visible for setup.
- Out-of-frame, wrong/ambiguous view, low visibility, low light, subject/camera movement, low FPS, and missing pose.
- Approximate movement phase and continuous rep cycles for profiles with a driver metric.
- Full-vs-partial movement under the configured geometric heuristic.
- Set completion from the plan target or duration target.

## What it cannot reliably detect

- Grip, load, bar path, bench angle, scapular motion, foot pressure, spinal segment detail, breathing, muscle tension, or intent.
- A clinically or biomechanically valid “bad form” judgement from 2D landmarks alone.
- Any rule that needs depth/occlusion information not present in the chosen view.
- Real-world accuracy across body proportions, lighting, camera models, clothing, and gym occlusion without device/video calibration.

## Synthetic behavioral cases executed

The audit contract tests independent acceptance behavior for: correct rep, acceptable small deviation, half rep, very fast rep, paused/stale cycle, low confidence, missing landmark, temporary occlusion, and a driver becoming unusable. The expected safe behavior is no false rep and no false warning when evidence is insufficient.

## What remains unprovable here

Flutter/Dart SDK and a real Android camera were unavailable in the sandbox. Therefore `flutter analyze`, `flutter test`, APK compilation, ML Kit camera rotation/format behavior, thermal behavior, FPS, and human biomechanics accuracy still require a real device/CI run.

## Practical device protocol

1. Test each Limited profile with 3 people, 2 body types, front/side placements, bright/dim light, and two Android devices.
2. For each profile perform 10 correct reps, 5 half reps, 5 deliberately fast reps, 5 pauses, and 5 landmark occlusions.
3. Record false full reps, missed reps, false warnings, time to Cannot Assess, FPS, inference time, CPU, memory, temperature, and battery.
4. Repeat with the phone moved during a set and with a second person entering the frame.
5. Accept a profile for stronger correction only after a labeled video set demonstrates low false-warning and missed-rep rates. Until then, keep it Tracking Only/Limited as shipped here.
